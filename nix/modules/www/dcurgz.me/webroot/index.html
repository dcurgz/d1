301 Moved Permanently

        I had intended this blog to be three things; a positive signal for
        employers, an interesting blog for computer enthusiasts, and a place to
        be authentic and vulnerable with my thoughts. 

        I quickly realized that the first intention was incompatible with the
        third, so I tried to divide my blog into two parts; on one domain,
        strictly technical posts that I could show to employers, and on the
        other, a place where I could express myself more freely.

        But this also wasn't working. I redrafted my first post many times, and
        never published it. My true thoughts and feelings on pertinent issues
        still bled through, and I was advised that this made it read
        unprofessional. 

        I can't do all three things, so I choose two; an interesting blog for
        computer enthusiasts, and a place where I can be authentic and
        vulnerable. Maybe I will make a formal professional blog at a later
        date, but right now it is not a priority for me.

        My blog is currently hosted at weirdfi.sh. Please feel free to visit.

        Sincerely,

        ~ d
